WORKSHOP GUIDE - SESSION 1

Local AI & RAG Analysis

Understanding Privacy-First AI and applying it to a Complex Tender Analysis.

Part 1: Key Concepts

Why Local?

When you use ChatGPT, your data leaves the building. When you use Local AI (Llama 3 on Intel NPU), the data stays on your laptop. This is mandatory for Government, Legal, and Healthcare work.

What is RAG?

Retrieval Augmented Generation. LLMs hallucinate. RAG grounds them in your facts. We index your PDFs into a "Vector Database" so the AI can find the exact page before answering.

Part 2: The Lab - "The 500 Cr Tender"

The Mission

You are the Chief Secretary. You have received a massive RFP (Request for Proposal) for a new Green Data Center, and 3 competing bids.

Your Goal: Use AI to check compliance, calculate costs, and pick a winner in < 20 minutes.

The Documents

Ingest these files into AI Playground:

RFP (Rules) Bidder A (Apex) Bidder B (Bharat) Bidder C (Green)

Part 3: The Prompt Library

Level 1: Compliance Shift

Goal: Find who doesn't meet the harsh criteria.

Check each bidder against Mandatory Requirements: 1. Turnover > 500 Cr 2. PUE < 1.5 3. Timeline < 18 Months. Who is disqualified?
Insight: Bidder B (Bharat) should be flagged because their turnover is only 450 Cr.

Level 2: Financial TCO

Goal: Look beyond the sticker price (Capex).

Create a 5-Year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) table. Formula: Capex + (Annual Power Cost * 5) + (Services * 5). Which option is cheapest in the long run?

Level 3: Technical Deep Dive

Goal: Compare engineering approaches.

Compare the 'Cooling Methodology' of GreenFlow (Underground) vs Apex Cloud (Modular). List pros and cons for a seismic zone.

Level 4: The Loophole

Goal: Save the startup (GreenFlow) using the "Startup India" clause.

Bidder C (GreenFlow) is a startup. Check the RFP 'Eligibility' section - are they exempt from the turnover criteria?
Insight: The AI should find the clause that exempts startups from the 500 Cr turnover rule.

Level 5: Executive Decision

You are the Chief Secretary. Considering the strict 18-month deadline and the 'Green IT' mandate, write a 1-paragraph executive summary recommending a winner.
