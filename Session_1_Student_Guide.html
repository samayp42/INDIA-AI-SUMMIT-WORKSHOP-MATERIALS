Key Concepts

The Privacy Problem

Cloud AI (ChatGPT, Gemini) sends your data to remote servers where it's stored and potentially used for training. For confidential documents—government files, medical records, legal contracts—this is unacceptable.

The Solution: Local AI

Run powerful AI models directly on your laptop. Zero internet required. Zero data leakage. Your documents never leave your device.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation — Instead of AI making up answers, RAG forces it to read YOUR document first, then answer based only on that content. Like an open-book exam. No hallucinations.

The Mission: ₹200 Cr Data Centre Tender Evaluation

You're a Senior Government Evaluator. The state government has released an RFP for a Tier-III Data Centre and three companies have submitted their bids. You have 15 minutes to analyse all four documents and recommend the best bidder. These are confidential documents — no cloud uploads allowed.

Step 3: Analyse with Prompts

Click any prompt to copy it. Paste into the chat and hit Enter.

📋 Tender Overview

Summarize the government tender requirements for the Data Centre project. What is the project scope, estimated budget ceiling, and delivery timeline?

🔧 Technical Specs

List all mandatory technical requirements from the tender — including Tier classification, uptime SLA, power redundancy, cooling specs, and network bandwidth. Format as a checklist.

🏢 Compare Bidders

Compare the three bidder proposals side by side. Create a table with columns: Company Name, Proposed Cost, Delivery Timeline, Key Strengths, Key Weaknesses.

✅ Compliance Matrix

For each bidder, check whether they meet ALL mandatory requirements from the tender. Flag any non-compliant items with the specific tender clause they violate.

💰 Cost Analysis

Analyze the cost breakdowns from all three bids. Which bidder offers the best value for money? Are there any hidden costs or items not included in their quotes?

🏆 Final Recommendation

Based on the tender evaluation criteria (technical capability 40%, cost 30%, timeline 20%, past experience 10%), rank the three bidders and recommend one with justification.
Verify Citations! The AI should reference specific sections from each document. Always cross-check the source text yourself — this catches any hallucinations.
