Key Concepts

The Privacy Problem

Cloud AI (ChatGPT, Gemini) sends your data to remote servers where it's stored and potentially used for training. For confidential documents—government files, medical records, legal contracts—this is unacceptable.

The Solution: Local AI

Run powerful AI models directly on your laptop. Zero internet required. Zero data leakage. Your documents never leave your device.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation — Instead of AI making up answers, RAG forces it to read YOUR document first, then answer based only on that content. Like an open-book exam. No hallucinations.

Hands-On Lab

The Mission: ₹500Cr Tender Analysis

You're a Senior Government Official. A 500-page confidential Smart City RFP landed on your desk. You have 15 minutes to extract critical info. You cannot upload it to any cloud service.

Step 1: Download Sample Document

Tender_Document_Secret.pdf

Step 2: Load into RAG

1

Open RAG Mode

In AI PC Immersion Lab, click "RAG" or "Chat with Documents" in the sidebar.

2

Upload Document

Click "Add Files" and select your PDF. Wait for "Vectorization Complete".

3

Start Querying

Use the prompts below to extract intelligence from the document.

Step 3: Query the Document

🎯 Executive Summary

Summarize this tender document. What is the project objective and estimated budget?

🔧 Technical Requirements

List all mandatory IoT sensor requirements from the Technical Specifications section. Format as bullet points.

⚠️ Compliance Check

Check for 'Data Localization' clauses. Does this tender require all data to be stored within India? Quote the exact text.

💰 Financial Risk

What are the penalty clauses for project delays exceeding 90 days? Is there a force majeure clause?
Verify Citations! The AI should cite page numbers like [Page 12]. Always click to verify the source text yourself—this catches any hallucinations.
