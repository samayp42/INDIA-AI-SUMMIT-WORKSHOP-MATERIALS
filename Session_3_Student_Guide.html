Understanding MCP

What is Model Context Protocol and why it matters

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is a standard way to connect AI models to external tools and data sources. Instead of the AI making things up, MCP lets it fetch real data from your Zerodha account, APIs, databases, and more.

Why Zerodha MCP?

The Zerodha MCP server connects AI Superbuilder directly to your Kite account. Ask questions in plain English and get real-time data from your actual portfolio, positions, and market quotes.

Mock Zerodha MCP — Fully Interactive! This workshop uses a mock Zerodha MCP server with simulated portfolio data. You can freely query holdings, positions, market data, and even place mock orders — everything works just like the real Kite API, but with sample data. Go ahead and experiment!

Getting Started

Initialize your mock account and explore the basics

Start Fresh Begin by asking the AI to initialize your account. This creates a mock portfolio with sample holdings and balance for you to play with.

Prompt 1: Initialize Account

Initialize my Zerodha mock account. Set me up with a fresh session so I can start trading.
The AI calls init() → creates a mock account with starting balance and sample holdings. You'll see your initial funds and portfolio.

Prompt 2: Check My Balance

What's my current account balance? How much cash do I have available for trading?
AI calls get-account() → returns your available funds, used margin, and total balance.

Prompt 3: View My Holdings

Show me all the stocks I currently own. For each stock, tell me the quantity, buy price, current price, and profit or loss.
AI calls get-holdings() → lists your portfolio with per-stock P&L breakdown.

Multi-Tool Thinking Chains

Watch the AI reason across multiple tools to solve complex queries

This is where it gets interesting! These prompts force the AI to call 2-4 tools in sequence, reason through intermediate results, and synthesize a final answer. Watch the thought chain carefully — this is agentic AI in action.

Prompt 11: News-Driven Trade

Check today's market news. Based on the sentiment and headlines, suggest one stock I should buy and one I should avoid. Then actually place a buy order for 5 shares of your recommended stock.
AI thinks: get-market-news() → analyze sentiment → get-quote() on candidates → reason about risk → place-order() for the pick. 3 tool calls chained with reasoning.

Prompt 12: Portfolio Health Check

Run a complete health check on my portfolio: show my holdings with current prices, calculate total P&L, check which sectors I'm concentrated in, and compare my performance against today's Nifty movement. Give me a risk score out of 10.
AI thinks: get-holdings() → get-quote() per stock → get-market-status() for Nifty → combine all data → synthesize risk assessment. 3+ tool calls.

Prompt 13: Smart Rebalancing

Look at my current holdings and account balance. I want to rebalance so that no single stock is more than 25% of my portfolio. Suggest specific sell and buy orders to achieve this, then execute them.
AI thinks: get-holdings() → get-account() → calculate weights → identify over-allocated stocks → place-order() sells → place-order() buys. Full agentic workflow.

Prompt 14: Historical Strategy Backtest

Get the 30-day price history for Reliance and TCS. Which stock had lower volatility? Which gave better returns? If I had ₹1 lakh to invest, how should I split between the two?
AI thinks: get-history() for Reliance → get-history() for TCS → calculate volatility & returns → recommend allocation. Analytical reasoning across data.

Prompt 15: Full Trading Day Simulation

Let's simulate a trading day. First check market status and news. Then check my account balance and holdings. Based on everything, make 3 smart trades (buys or sells) and explain your reasoning for each. Finally, show my updated portfolio.
AI orchestrates 6+ tool calls: get-market-status() → get-market-news() → get-account() → get-holdings() → 3x place-order() → get-holdings(). Full agent loop.
