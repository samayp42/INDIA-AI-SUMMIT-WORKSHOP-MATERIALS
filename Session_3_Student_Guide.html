Connect AI to your Zerodha trading account. Get portfolio insights, analyze positions, and explore market data using natural language.

1

Understanding MCP

What is Model Context Protocol and why it matters

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is a standard way to connect AI models to external tools and data sources. Instead of the AI making things up, MCP lets it fetch real data from your Zerodha account, APIs, databases, and more.

Why Zerodha MCP?

The Zerodha MCP server connects AI Superbuilder directly to your Kite account. Ask questions in plain English and get real-time data from your actual portfolio, positions, and market quotes.

Important: Read-Only Mode The Zerodha MCP is configured in read-only mode for safety. It can VIEW your portfolio and market data but CANNOT place, modify, or cancel orders. Your funds are completely safe.
2

Zerodha MCP Tools

All the capabilities available through the MCP connection

The Zerodha MCP exposes these tools that AI can call on your behalf:

Holdings

View your long-term stock holdings with P&L

get_holdings()
Positions

View intraday and F&O positions

get_positions()
Margins

Check available trading margins

get_margins()
Quote

Get live price for any instrument

get_quote(symbol)
Orders

View today's order history

get_orders()
Historical Data

Get OHLC candles for analysis

get_historical()

Setup on AI Superbuilder

Connect Zerodha MCP to Intel AI Superbuilder

Connection Steps

1
Open AI Superbuilder

Launch Intel AI Superbuilder application on your AI PC.

2
Navigate to MCP Settings

Go to Settings → MCP Servers → Add New Server

3
Configure Zerodha MCP

Enter the MCP server details provided in the workshop:

Server URL: localhost:3000/zerodha-mcp
4
Authenticate with Kite

You'll be redirected to Zerodha login. Enter your credentials to authorize the connection.

5
Verify Connection

You should see "Zerodha MCP: Connected" with a green indicator.

Connection Successful! Once connected, you can ask natural language questions about your portfolio and the AI will automatically call the appropriate Zerodha MCP tools.
4

Portfolio Queries

Analyze your holdings and positions with natural language

Holdings

Query 1: Portfolio Overview

Show me my complete portfolio holdings. What's my total invested value and current value?
The AI will call get_holdings() and display your stocks with buy price, current price, quantity, and P&L for each.

Query 2: Top Performers

Which stocks in my portfolio have given me the best returns? Show top 5 by percentage gain.

Query 3: Loss-Making Stocks

List all stocks in my portfolio that are currently in loss. What's the total unrealized loss?
Positions

Query 4: Intraday Positions

Show my current intraday positions. Am I in profit or loss today?

Query 5: Sector Exposure

Analyze my portfolio by sector. Which sector has the highest allocation?
5

Market Data Queries

Get real-time quotes and historical data

Live Quotes

Query 6: Single Stock Quote

What is the current price of Reliance Industries? Show me the day's high, low, and volume.

Query 7: Multiple Stocks Comparison

Compare the current prices of TCS, Infosys, and Wipro. Which IT stock is performing best today?

Query 8: Index Status

What's the current Nifty 50 level? Is it up or down from yesterday's close?
Historical Analysis

Query 9: Price History

Get the last 30 days price data for HDFC Bank. What was the highest and lowest price in this period?

Query 10: Trend Analysis

Analyze the 1-month trend for Tata Motors. Is it in an uptrend or downtrend? Calculate the percentage change.
6

Advanced Analysis

Complex multi-step queries combining multiple data sources

Multi-Tool Queries These queries require the AI to call multiple MCP tools and combine the results. Watch the "Thought Chain" to see how the AI reasons through each step.

Query 11: Portfolio vs Market

Compare my portfolio's performance against Nifty 50 today. Am I beating the market or underperforming?

Query 12: Concentration Risk

Analyze my portfolio concentration. Is any single stock more than 20% of my total portfolio? What's the risk?

Query 13: Margin Utilization

Check my available margin. How much more can I invest today? What percentage of my margin is already used?

Query 14: P&L Summary

Give me a complete P&L summary: total invested, current value, absolute profit/loss, and percentage return. Also show today's gain/loss separately.

Query 15: Investment Suggestion

Based on my current holdings and available margin, which sectors am I underexposed to? Suggest areas to diversify.

  • Zerodha MCP provides read-only access to your Kite account
  • Natural language queries automatically translate to API calls
  • Multi-step reasoning allows complex portfolio analysis
  • AI can combine multiple data sources for comprehensive insights
