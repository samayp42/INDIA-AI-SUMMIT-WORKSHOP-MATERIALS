Part 3: The Prompt Library

Level 1: The Modern Base

Goal: Establish a realistic baseline.

Modern luxury living room, floor to ceiling windows, overlooking forest, soft morning light, architectural digest style, 8k, photorealistic, cinematic lighting.

Level 2: The Trash Filter (Negative)

Goal: Remove artifacts.

text, watermark, bad anatomy, distorted perspective, extra furniture, dark, gloomy, low quality, jpeg artifacts, blur

Level 3: The Industrial Variant

Goal: Change materials (Brick & Steel).

Industrial loft apartment, exposed brick walls, steel beams, edison bulbs, leather sofa, concrete floor, warehouse conversion style, dramatic shadows.

Level 4: The Cyberpunk Pivot

Goal: Total style change while keeping layout.

Cyberpunk apartment interior, neon lights, holographic screens, rain on window, blade runner aesthetic, dark atmosphere, purple and blue lighting.
ControlNet Tip: Set ControlNet to "Canny" with weight 1.0 to preserve the sketch lines perfectly.
