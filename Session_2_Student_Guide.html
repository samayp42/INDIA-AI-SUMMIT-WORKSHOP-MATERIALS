The Mission

A client sends a rough napkin sketch of their dream living room. They want to see 3 style variations before committing to ₹15 Lakh renovation. You have 10 minutes.

How to Enable Image-to-Image In AI PC Immersion Lab → Select "Image to Image" mode → Upload your sketch (JPG/PNG) → Enter your prompt

Video Generation

Create 4-second AI video clips for social media & commercials

The Mission

Create a product reveal video for Instagram. No camera crew. No editing software. No budget.

Video 1: Product Reveal

VIDEO PROMPT
// Product Reveal Video { "action": "Smartphone emerging from elegant white packaging", "camera": "slow zoom in, subtle rotation", "lighting": "soft studio lights, subtle reflections", "background": "clean white, minimal", "style": "Apple commercial aesthetic, premium feel", "quality": "cinematic, smooth motion, 4K" }

Smartphone emerging from elegant white packaging, slow zoom in, subtle rotation, soft studio lights, subtle reflections, clean white minimal background, Apple commercial aesthetic, premium feel, cinematic, smooth motion, 4K

Video 2: Car Showroom

VIDEO PROMPT
// Automotive Commercial { "subject": "Luxury sports car in dark showroom", "camera": "smooth orbit around the car, 360 rotation", "lighting": "dramatic spotlight, reflections on polished floor", "atmosphere": "premium, mysterious, sleek", "style": "automotive commercial, cinematic", "quality": "high-end production, smooth motion" }

Luxury sports car in dark showroom, smooth orbit camera rotation around the car, dramatic spotlight lighting, reflections on polished floor, premium mysterious sleek atmosphere, automotive commercial, cinematic, high-end production, smooth motion

Video 3: Coffee Commercial

VIDEO PROMPT
// Beverage Commercial { "action": "Coffee being poured into premium ceramic cup", "details": "steam rising elegantly, slow motion pour", "lighting": "warm cafe lighting, soft bokeh background", "mood": "cozy, inviting, morning warmth", "style": "food commercial, advertising quality", "quality": "cinematic slow motion, rich colors" }
Strength slider explained: Lower values (0.3–0.5) keep the image closer to your original sketch. Higher values (0.7–0.9) give the AI more creative freedom. Start at 0.7 and adjust.

Video Generation Settings

4 seconds
Fixed output length
2-5 minutes
Longer than images
Smooth camera moves
Complex human motion
Video Pro Tips
  • Focus on camera movement (orbit, zoom, pan) rather than complex actions
  • Products and objects work better than people
  • For longer videos, generate multiple 4-sec clips and stitch them

What You Learned

  • Structured prompts = better quality (Subject → Setting → Lighting → Style → Quality)
  • Negative prompts are essential to avoid common issues
  • Image-to-Image transforms sketches into photorealistic renders
  • Strength slider controls how much AI changes your input
  • Video generation works best with camera movements & products
