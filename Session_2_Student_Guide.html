Creative AI Studio

Generate professional images, transform sketches into photorealistic renders, and create AI videos — all locally on your Intel AI PC, at zero cost.

Part 1: Key Concepts

What is a Diffusion Model?

Diffusion models start with random noise and gradually refine it into a coherent image through ~50 "denoising" steps, guided by your text prompt. Think of it like sculpting — starting with a rough block and carving details with each pass.

Key takeaway: The entire model runs on your GPU/NPU. No cloud API. No per-image fees. Unlimited generations.

Text-to-Image vs Image-to-Image

Text-to-Image: You describe what you want in words and the AI creates it from scratch. Great for product shots, concept art, and marketing visuals.

Image-to-Image (Img2Img): You upload a reference image (sketch, photo) and the AI transforms it while keeping the original structure. Perfect for converting sketches into photorealistic renders.

AI Video Generation

Same principle as image generation, but extended to create short 4-second video clips. Describe a scene, and the AI generates a video with motion, lighting, and camera angles. Ideal for product reveals, social media content, and concept videos.

Mode Image-to-Image

Strength slider explained: Lower values (0.3–0.5) keep the image closer to your original sketch. Higher values (0.7–0.9) give the AI more creative freedom. Start at 0.7 and adjust.
