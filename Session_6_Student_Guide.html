PART 1

The Big Picture — Why IoT + AI?

💡

The Core Idea

What if your AI assistant could see, feel, and act on the real world — not just answer questions from the internet? That's what happens when you connect an IoT device to an LLM.

In this capstone session, you'll bridge the gap between the digital world (LLMs, AI agents) and the physical world (air quality sensors, environmental data). Instead of the AI only reading text, it will read real sensor data — temperature, humidity, PM2.5, CO₂ — in real-time.

What You'll Use Today

AI Superbuilder

Your AI interface — a powerful chat environment pre-connected to LLMs. This is where you'll type prompts and see the AI respond with real sensor intelligence.

AIrowl MCP Server

The bridge between AI and IoT. This MCP server exposes AirOwl sensor data as "tools" that the LLM can call — just like a function call, but to a physical device.

AirOwl Device

A real air quality monitor measuring PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, TVOC, Temperature, and Humidity. It sits on your desk and feeds live data to the AI.

MCP (Model Context Protocol)

The open standard that lets AI models call external tools and data sources. Think of it as a USB port for AI — plug in any capability.

PART 4

The Mission — Smart City Engineer

🏙️ Your Role: Smart City Air Quality Analyst

You've been assigned to monitor the air quality at a school campus in Delhi during winter. Construction is happening nearby. Your job is to use the AI + AirOwl system to fetch live data, analyze trends, generate health advisories, and control the monitoring display — all through natural language.

PART 5

The Prompt Lab — 6 Levels

Work through these prompts in order. Each level builds on the previous one and teaches a new AI + IoT skill.

1

Live Status Check

Beginner
🎯 Goal: Make the AI fetch real-time sensor data. Learn how the LLM triggers a tool call.
🔧 get_pm25 🔧 get_temp 🔧 get_humd
Prompt 1.1 — Single Reading
What is the current PM2.5 level from the AirOwl sensor? Is it safe to breathe right now?
Prompt 1.2 — Multi-Parameter
Fetch the current temperature, humidity, and PM2.5 from the AirOwl. Give me a quick summary of the indoor environment quality.

👀 Watch For:

Notice in the AI's response how it calls get_pm25(), get_temp(), etc. The LLM decided which tools to call based on your question. That's the magic of MCP.

2

Health Advisory

Beginner
🎯 Goal: Make the AI interpret the data against health standards. This is AI reasoning over IoT data.
🔧 get_pm25 🔧 get_co2 🔧 get_tvoc
Prompt 2.1 — School Safety
Read the PM2.5 and CO₂ levels from AirOwl. Based on the WHO and Indian NAQI standards, is it safe for children to play outside right now? If not, what specific precautions should the school take?
Prompt 2.2 — Classroom Ventilation
Check the CO₂ and TVOC levels. If CO₂ is above 1000 ppm, recommend how many windows the classroom should open. Factor in the current outdoor PM2.5 — if that's also high, suggest an alternative solution.

Learning: The AI doesn't just return a number — it cross-references with health guidelines, considers trade-offs (ventilation vs. outdoor pollution), and gives actionable advice. That's the power of LLM reasoning on real data.

3

Screen Control — AI Meets Hardware

Intermediate
🎯 Goal: Command the AI to change the AirOwl's physical display. This is the AI controlling a device, not just reading from it.
🔧 change_screen 🔧 get_pm25 🔧 get_co2
Prompt 3.1 — Simple Display Change
Switch the AirOwl screen to show the PM2.5 graph so visitors in the lobby can see it.
Prompt 3.2 — Intelligent Display
Check all air quality parameters. Whichever one is currently the most concerning (highest relative to its safety threshold), switch the AirOwl screen to display that parameter's graph.

🖥️ Look at the Device!

After running Prompt 3.2, look at the AirOwl screen on your desk. The AI just decided which graph matters most and changed the display. You controlled hardware with a conversation.

4

Trend Analysis — Historical Reasoning

Intermediate
🎯 Goal: Use historical data to identify patterns, anomalies, and trends. This is where AI stops being a dashboard and becomes an analyst.
🔧 get_historical_pm25 🔧 get_historical_co2 🔧 change_screen
Prompt 4.1 — Trend Detection
Pull the historical PM2.5 data. Has air quality been getting better or worse over time? Identify any spikes and suggest what might have caused them (traffic, construction, weather).
Prompt 4.2 — Anomaly Hunting
Get the historical CO₂ data. Are there any sudden spikes? If so, what time did they occur and what could explain them? Then show the CO₂ graph on the AirOwl screen.

Notice: The AI is now multi-stepping — first fetching historical data, then analyzing patterns, then controlling the screen. A single prompt triggered 3 tool calls. This is agentic behavior.

5

Report Generation — AI as Your Analyst

Advanced
🎯 Goal: Make the AI combine live + historical data into a professional document. This is AI doing real work.
🔧 get_pm25 🔧 get_pm10 🔧 get_co2 🔧 get_tvoc 🔧 get_temp 🔧 get_humd 🔧 get_historical_pm25
Prompt 5.1 — Formal Safety Report
You are an environmental safety officer. Fetch ALL current air quality readings (PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, TVOC, Temperature, Humidity) and the historical PM2.5 trend. Generate a formal "Daily Air Quality Report" for the school principal with: 1) Current status summary 2) Risk level (Green/Yellow/Red) 3) Trend analysis 4) Specific recommendations for today.
Prompt 5.2 — Complaint Letter
The PM2.5 has been consistently high. Pull the historical PM2.5 data as evidence, then draft a formal complaint letter to the Municipal Corporation about nearby construction dust affecting our school's air quality. Include the actual data readings as evidence.
6

Full Agent Mode — Sensor Diagnostics

Expert
🎯 Goal: Make the AI reason about sensor health, detect faults, and troubleshoot like an engineer.
🔧 All Tools
Prompt 6.1 — Sensor Health Check
Run a full diagnostic on the AirOwl sensor. Fetch every available parameter. Check if any readings are physically impossible (e.g., negative values, humidity > 100%, PM2.5 = 0 with PM10 > 200). Report the sensor's health status and flag any anomalies that suggest a hardware issue.
Prompt 6.2 — Smart Dashboard
Act as a smart monitoring system. Check all current readings, then check historical trends. Based on your analysis: 1) Identify the most critical parameter right now 2) Switch the AirOwl screen to show that parameter 3) Explain why you chose it 4) Set a "risk score" from 1-10 for the overall environment.

🚀 Full Autonomy

Prompt 6.2 is a fully autonomous AI agent: it reads, analyzes, decides, and acts — all from a single prompt. Count how many tool calls it makes!

PART 6

Bonus Challenges

🏆

Think You've Mastered It? Try These.

These open-ended challenges push your prompt engineering skills further. No right answers — just creative problem-solving.

Challenge A — The Comparator
Compare the current PM2.5 with the historical average. Is today better or worse than usual? By what percentage? Present this as a headline a news anchor would read.
Challenge B — The Correlator
Fetch historical PM2.5 and CO₂ data. Do these two parameters correlate (rise and fall together)? If yes, what does that tell us about the pollution source — is it combustion (traffic/industry) or dust?
Challenge C — The Storyteller
You are a journalist writing a story about air pollution in Indian schools. Use ALL the AirOwl data — live and historical — to write a 200-word news article. Include real numbers from the sensor. End with a call to action.
