Professional AI Editing: Inpainting, Masking, and Upscaling.

Part 1: Key Concepts

Inpainting (Masking)

Inpainting is the art of "filling holes". You select an area (the hole/mask), and the AI hallucinates new content to fit seamlessly, using the surrounding pixels as context.

Super Resolution

Upscaling a low-res image by 4x. Unlike traditional resizing (which gets blurry), AI Super Resolution hallucinates new details like skin pores and brick textures to make it sharp.

Part 2: The Lab - "Photo Rescue"

The Mission

A client has given you a damaged, low-resolution vacation photo. Your job is to remove the scratches, add a cool accessory, change the background, and upscale it to 4K for printing.
