AI Photo Editing

Master GIMP + OpenVINO for Professional Inpainting, Object Removal & Super Resolution

PART 1

Understanding AI Editing

OpenVINO brings AI-powered editing directly into GIMP, enabling professional-grade photo manipulation that was previously only possible with expensive cloud services.

Inpainting

AI "fills the hole" - select an area, and AI generates matching content using surrounding context. Perfect for removing unwanted objects.

Generative Fill

Add new elements to your image. Select an area and describe what you want - AI creates it seamlessly matching lighting and style.

Super Resolution

Upscale images by 4x while adding realistic detail. AI generates textures, sharpness, and clarity that simple resizing cannot.

OpenVINO Acceleration

All processing happens locally on your Intel hardware (CPU/GPU/NPU) - no internet needed, complete privacy.

PART 3

Technique 1: Inpainting (Remove Objects)

Remove unwanted elements from your photos - people, objects, text, scratches, or any distracting element.

REMOVE Object/Person Removal

Step-by-Step Workflow:

  1. Select the Free Select Tool (Lasso) from the toolbox
  2. Draw around the object you want to remove (include a small margin)
  3. Go to Layer → OpenVINO-AI-Plugins → Stable Diffusion
  4. Select Inpainting mode in the dialog
  5. Enter your prompt and click Generate
Prompt for Clean Removal
clean background, natural texture, seamless blend, photorealistic, matching lighting
Denoising Strength 0.75 - 0.85
Steps 20 - 30
CFG Scale 7 - 9

Pro Tip: Select slightly MORE than the object to give AI enough context for seamless blending.

PART 5

Technique 3: Background Replacement

Transport subjects to entirely new locations by replacing the background while keeping the subject intact.

TRANSFORM Background Swap

Workflow:

  1. Use Free Select Tool to select the background (not the subject)
  2. Or use Fuzzy Select for simpler backgrounds
  3. Go to Layer → OpenVINO-AI-Plugins → Stable Diffusion → Inpainting
  4. Describe your new background

PART 6

Technique 4: Super Resolution

Upscale your images by 4x while adding realistic detail - perfect for printing or recovering low-res photos.

UPSCALE 4x Super Resolution

Simple Workflow:

  1. Open your low-resolution image in GIMP
  2. Go to Layer → OpenVINO-AI-Plugins → Super Resolution
  3. Select 4x Upscale option
  4. Click Run - no prompt needed!
  5. Wait for processing (may take a minute depending on image size)

No Prompt Required! Super Resolution automatically analyzes your image and adds appropriate detail. Just select the option and run.
