Upload a rough hand-drawn sketch. The AI converts it into a photorealistic render using Image-to-Image (Img2Img).

// Steps in AI PC Immersion Lab:
1. Switch to "Image to Image" mode
2. Upload your sketch (JPG/PNG)
3. Enter prompt:
"Transform this living room sketch into
a photorealistic modern interior, natural
lighting, premium furniture, wooden
flooring, architectural visualization, 4K"
4. Set Strength: 0.6-0.8
// Lower = closer to sketch
// Higher = more creative freedom
