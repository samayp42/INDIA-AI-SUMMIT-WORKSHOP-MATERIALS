Concept

The Latency Budget

Humans expect a reply in < 500ms.

If STT takes 200ms, and LLM takes 500ms, and TTS takes 300ms... that's 1 second. Too slow!

We must optimize every step.

The Mission

Scenario: The Newsroom

You are a Producer.

The Task: Create a lively, fast-talking News Anchor Bot to interview you about "Tech Trends".

Tools: Pipecat + Whisper + Llama 3.

News Anchor AI

Step 1

System Prompt

Goal: Define the Persona.

"You are a fast-paced, witty Tech News Anchor named 'Cyber Sam'. Keep answers short (under 2 sentences). Interview the user about the latest AI breakthroughs. Be energetic!"
Step 2

Optimizing for Speed

Goal: Reduce Latency.

1. STT Model: 'Whisper Tiny' (Fastest) 2. VAD Sensitivity: High (Don't wait for silence) 3. TTS Speed: 1.2x (News Anchors talk fast!)
Step 3

Go Live!

Start the conversation.

Observe if the AI interrupts you (VAD issue) or if it's too slow (Latency issue).

You: "Sam, what do you think about AI Agents?"

Sam: "Agents are the future! But are they safe? Let's discuss."

Step 4

Performance Review

Check the console logs.

[INFO] STT: 150ms [INFO] LLM First Token: 300ms [INFO] TTS Audio Ready: 100ms Total Latency: 550ms (Excellent)
Summary

Key Takeaways

  • VAD: Voice Activity Detection is crucial for natural turns.
  • Speed > Quality: For voice, we prefer a fast "Dumb" model over a slow "Smart" one.
  • Multimodal: Combining STT+LLM+TTS is the ultimate test.
