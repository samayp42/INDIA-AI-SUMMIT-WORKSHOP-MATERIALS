Pro Tip

More Steps =
Better Quality

The "Steps" setting controls how many times the AI refines the image. More refinements = more detail and accuracy.

10 Steps — Fast, rough ⚡ ~5s
20 Steps — Good balance ⚡ ~12s
30 Steps — Best quality ✨ ⚡ ~20s
50 Steps — Diminishing returns ⚡ ~35s
🎯 Sweet spot: 20-30 steps for most edits. Above 30, quality gains are minimal but time doubles.
