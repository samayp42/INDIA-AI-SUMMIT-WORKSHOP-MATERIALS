AI Photo Editing

Concepts: Inpainting, Masking, and Super Resolution in GIMP.

Concept

How Inpainting Works

The AI looks at the context pixels around a hole (mask) and predicts what should be inside.

It's like advanced "Content-Aware Fill" but it understands objects, not just textures.

The Mask (What AI fills)

Concept

OpenVINO Integration

GIMP is a classic editor. We inject "AI Brains" into it using the OpenVINO plugin.

This allows GIMP to run Stable Diffusion directly on your NPU.

The Mission

Scenario: Restoration

You are a Photo Editor.

The Task: Restore a damaged family photo and change the background.

Tools: GIMP + OpenVINO Plugin.

Project: "Memories"

Step 1

Remove Defects

Task: Select the scratch/tear on the photo.

Action: 1. Use Lasso Tool to select the scratch. 2. Open AI Plugin -> Inpaint Mode. 3. Prompt: "clean background, remove scratch"

(The AI fills the gap seamlessly.)

Step 2

Add Objects

Task: Add sunglasses to the person.

Action: 1. Select the eyes area. 2. Prompt: "Cool aviator sunglasses, reflection" 3. Strength: 0.8

(Be careful with the strength slider!)

Step 3

Swap Background

Task: Change the location to Paris.

Action: 1. Invert Selection (Select Background). 2. Prompt: "Eiffel tower in background, blurred, sunny day, paris street"
Step 4

Upscale (Super Res)

Task: Make it printable (4K).

Action: 1. Open AI Plugin -> Super Resolution Mode. 2. Factor: 4x 3. Run on GPU.

Turn a 512px image into a 2048px poster instantly.

Summary

Workflow

  • Selection is Key: Good masks = Good results.
  • Non-Destructive: Use layers.
  • Local: No cloud credits wasted on 50 iterations.
