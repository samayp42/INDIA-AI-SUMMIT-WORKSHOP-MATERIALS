Wiring (ESP32)

We need 3 Wires:

  • VCC (Red): Power (3.3V)
  • GND (Black): Ground
  • DATA (Yellow): The Signal (Pin 21)

The Mission

Scenario: AirOwl

You are a Smart City Engineer.

The Task: Deploy an Air Quality Monitor and use AI to advise citizens.

Tools: ESP32, PMS5003 Sensor, Python MCP.

Project AirOwl

Step 1

The Payload

The sensor sends this JSON every second.

{ "device_id": "AirOwl_01", "pm25": 145, "humidity": 65, "status": "online" }

(PM2.5 of 145 is very high!)

Step 2

AI Consultation

Ask the AI to interpret the raw numbers.

"Fetch the sensor data from AirOwl. Based on the PM2.5 level, is it safe to organize a marathon outdoors today?"
Step 3

Recommendation Engine

The AI acts as a health expert.

AI Response: "The PM2.5 is 145, which is Unhealthy. Recommendation: Cancel the marathon. Suggest indoor activities. Participants should wear N95 masks."
Step 4

Detecting Anomalies

Goal: Spot broken sensors.

"If PM2.5 is 0 but Humidity is 0, is the sensor working? Debug this."
Summary

IoT + AI Power

  • Real-Time: AI decisions on live physical data.
  • Edge Computing: Process locally, save bandwidth.
  • Actionable: Raw numbers -> Health Advice.
